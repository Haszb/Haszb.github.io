I work across data, AI, project management and writing, from framing a problem to building a solution and explaining the decisions behind it.

Before moving into data and AI, I spent several years coordinating projects and managing tenders across various sectors, such as security, cosmetics or maintenance. Working across these environments taught me the importance of translating operational needs into clearly defined problems that can be assessed and addressed. In security, I also encountered practical applications of image recognition, which contributed to my decision to make the transition.

In 2024, I completed a Master’s-level program in Machine Learning Engineering & AI Project Management at DataScientest (now Liora), certified by Mines Paris PSL.

I’m currently developing Rochondra independently, from document collection and processing to pipeline design and architectural decisions. The project brings together nearly 6,000 cryptocurrency whitepapers and a public decision log documenting the choices and trade-offs behind the system.

This site gathers my projects and writing.

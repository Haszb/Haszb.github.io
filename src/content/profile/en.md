I work at the intersection of data, AI and project management, from framing a problem to building a solution, and I justify the choices that shape it.

Before moving into data and AI, I spent several years coordinating projects and leading tenders across various sectors, notably security, cosmetics and maintenance. These roles taught me to translate operational needs into clearly framed problems that can be assessed and addressed. It was also in security that I discovered practical applications of image recognition, which prompted my career change.

In 2024, I completed a Master’s-level program in Machine Learning Engineering & AI Project Management at DataScientest (now Liora), certified by Mines Paris PSL.

I’m currently developing Rochondra, from document collection and processing to pipeline design and architectural decisions. The project brings together nearly 6,000 crypto project whitepapers, alongside a public decision log in which I document my choices and the trade-offs they involve.

This site brings together my projects and writing.
